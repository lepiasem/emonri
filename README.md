# Emonri

Emonri is a desktop app for **Windows and macOS** that helps people and families organize, browse, and enjoy the photos and videos they already own.

With Emonri, you can choose your media folders, browse and play photos and videos, and find forgotten moments in the folders you choose. You can also create Stories from your photos and videos, edit Notes, and share content with other devices on your own Wi-Fi network.

## What you can do

- Choose media folders from local folders, USB drives, or network/NAS drives
- Find moments across your collection
- Browse and play your photos and videos
- Create Stories to organize selected media with text
- Edit Notes
- Share content with other devices on your own Wi-Fi network
